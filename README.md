# HR_ANALYTICS_DASHBOARD_PROJECT
# HR Attrition & Employee Demographics Analysis Dashboard (Microsoft Excel)

## Executive Summary
This project analyzes employee attrition trends within an enterprise dataset of 1,470 employees. The primary objective is to identify key risk drivers behind workforce turnover, evaluate demographic and department-level patterns, and present actionable insights to HR leadership to improve employee retention strategies.

---

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
* **Total Workforce:** 1,470 Employees
* **Total Attrition Count:** 237 Employees
* **Active Employees:** 1,233 Employees
* **Overall Attrition Rate:** 16.1%
* **Average Employee Age:** 37 Years

---

## Primary Insights & Business Findings
1. **Department Vulnerability:** Research & Development (R&D) exhibits the highest absolute turnover with 133 employee departures (56% of total attrition), followed by Sales with 92 departures (39%).
2. **Career Stage Impact:** The 25–34 age demographic represents the highest turnover cluster (112 employees total), indicating critical drop-off during mid-career transition periods.
3. **Role Specific Attrition:** Laboratory Technicians (62 departures) and Sales Executives (57 departures) represent the top two specific job roles affected by employee turnover.
4. **Marital Status Dynamics:** Single employees account for over 50% of total departures (120 out of 237), showing higher mobility compared to married or divorced cohorts.

---

## Excel Architecture & Technical Implementation
* **Pivot Tables & Calculated Fields:** Built dynamic pivot aggregations to calculate slice-and-dice attrition metrics across dimensions.
* **Interactive Slicers:** Integrated dynamic Slicers for **Gender**, **Education Level**, **Job Role**, and **Marital Status** to enable interactive visual filtering.
* **Advanced Formulas:** Utilized `SUM`, `AVERAGE`, `COUNTIFS`, and dynamic `DIVIDE` logical structures for KPI KPI cards.
* **Custom Visualizations:** Designed horizontal bar charts, donut charts, tree maps, and grouped column charts to highlight key employee metrics.
